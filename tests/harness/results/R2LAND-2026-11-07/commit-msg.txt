DOS-PORT: patch 0369 -- lever R2: an unchanged character reuses its prepared image and skips its restore, blit and present (DOSAGS_CHAR_REUSE, default off)

Why: in Maniac Mansion Deluxe room 1 the three kids are drawn at 100% zoom with no tint or light, two of them never
change and Dave is idle in 88.5% of frames, yet every frame, for every kid, the engine re-copies the cached image into
the raw bitmap, re-runs the walk-behind crop-out, marks the old and new rects (so the background is restored under
them), masked-blits the sprite back and presents the box (dosags docs/lab/LAB-R2.md sections 0 and 6). Character work
is 2.83 ms of a 10.29 ms room-1 drawn tick at DOSBox-X fixed 40000; LAB-R2B measured R2 at 10.29 -> 7.42 ms busy.

What: DOSAGS_CHAR_REUSE unset or 0 = off (the 0368 head; the default); 1 = R2a; 2 = R2a + R2b. Software renderer only.
 R2a: when a character's whole input key equals last scene's (sprite and mirror, view/loop/frame, the six tint/light
  fields, zoom, width/height, final position and baseline, transparency, the no-walk-behinds flag,
  walk_behind_baselines_changed, the walk-behind mask generation, room, background frame) and its raw bitmap and
  texture are the ones last scene left, construct_object_gfx's cached branch returns "intact": no re-copy, no
  crop-out, no texture sync. A dynamic sprite edited in place is caught by construct_object_gfx's own cache test
  (IsChangeNotified, set by notify_sprite_changed), which R2a's branch sits behind. The mask generation is new against
  the lab lever (LAB-R2B): a DrawingSurface on the walk-behind mask changes what the crop-out reads without touching
  walk_behind_baselines_changed, so it is bumped by walkbehinds_recalc() and by every walk-behind DrawingSurface access
  (as 0319 does for the walkable mask).
 R2b (mode 2): a character that took R2a, was composited last scene and composites idempotently (transparency 0, no
  alpha channel) skips its mark, like 0233's room overlays, and is flagged to the driver like 0169's GUI entries, so
  0368 draws it only inside the dirty boxes that reach it. Every DOSAGS_GUI_CLIP mode has a path that redraws such an
  entry wherever the restore repainted (4 per box, 2 and 1 the bbox, 0 unflagged and drawn in full), so no mode refuses
  R2b.
Guards: (1) a character left unmarked and now off, in another room, or not key-same (baseline included) gets its OLD
rect marked before this scene's restore (0197's convention), with a net at the end of the scene; (2) drawn in full,
not skipped, under 0366's latch, on a camera or viewport move, on a full invalidation (dos_port_dirty full or its
carry), under 0368's coarse flag, or with more than one room viewport (counted before the first one is drawn); (3)
every record dropped on clear_drawobj_cache, a room change, or a scene without exactly one composite.
Witness: "DOS-PORT charreuse DOSAGS_CHAR_REUSE=<v|(default)> mode=<n>" at driver construction; "DOS-PORT
charreuse_final ..." in RUNMANI.LOG in both flavours. DOSAGS_CHAR_REUSE joins the RUNMANIFEST env allowlist.
